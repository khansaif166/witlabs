## What a good website design company should actually deliver

A website is not just pages and colours. For a small or growing business, it has one job: turn a stranger into an enquiry. A good website design company in Bangalore should give you these six things as standard.

1. **Mobile-first design.** Most of your visitors will open your site on a phone, and Google itself [uses the mobile version of your site](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/mobile/mobile-sites-mobile-first-indexing) to decide where you rank. If it is slow or hard to tap on mobile, people leave.
2. **Fast loading.** A page that takes 8 to 10 seconds to load loses people before they see your offer. Ask for a site that loads in about 3 seconds on mobile data, and test any agency's past work yourself on [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/).
3. **Clear ways to contact you.** A tap-to-call phone number, a WhatsApp button and a short contact form on every page. Not hidden at the bottom of the About page.
4. **Basic SEO built in.** Proper page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text and a sitemap, as described in [Google's SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide). Without this, Google does not know what your pages are about.
5. **Google Business Profile setup.** For local businesses in Bangalore, your [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/) often brings more calls than your website. The two should work together.
6. **You own everything.** The domain should be registered in your name, and you should get the login details. If the agency disappears, your website should not.

The sticker price is not the full story. Before you compare quotes, check what each one includes:

- Is the domain and hosting included, and for how long?
- How many pages, and how many rounds of changes?
- Is basic SEO included, or is it an extra charge?
- What does it cost to maintain the site after launch?

A ₹10,000 quote that charges extra for hosting, SEO and every small change can end up costing more than a ₹15,000 quote that includes them.

## 7 questions to ask before hiring a website design company in Bangalore

Ask these on the first call. A good agency will answer them clearly. A weak one will change the subject to design.

1. **"Can I see websites you built for businesses like mine?"** Ask for live links, not screenshots. Open them on your phone while you are on the call.
2. **"Did those websites bring the client more enquiries?"** You are not buying a design. You are buying calls, forms and WhatsApp messages. Ask what changed for their clients after launch.
3. **"Whose name is the domain registered in?"** It must be yours. You can check who owns any domain with [ICANN's free lookup tool](https://lookup.icann.org/). This one question saves a lot of trouble later.
4. **"What exactly is included in this price?"** Pages, revisions, hosting, SEO, content writing, images. Get it in writing.
5. **"How long will it take, and what do you need from me?"** Most delays happen because the business owner has not sent the content. A good agency tells you upfront what they need and when.
6. **"What happens after launch?"** Who fixes things if the site breaks? What do updates cost? Is there a maintenance plan?
7. **"Will my website show up on Google?"** Nobody can promise the number one spot. But they should explain how they will set up SEO and your Google Business Profile so people can find you.

## Red flags when hiring a web design agency in Bangalore

- **"We guarantee first page on Google."** No honest company can guarantee a ranking. [Google's own guide to hiring an SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo) says: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google."
- **No live portfolio.** Only mockups or screenshots, and no websites you can actually open.
- **The domain is registered in their name.** This makes it hard to move your website later.
- **No written scope.** If what is included is only discussed on a call, expect surprise charges later.
- **Very low price with no explanation.** A ₹3,000 website is usually a template with your logo on it, with no SEO and no support.
- **They never ask about your business.** If they do not ask who your customers are or where your enquiries come from today, they are building a brochure, not a business tool.

## FAQs about website design companies in Bangalore

### How much does a website cost in Bangalore?

A basic business website usually costs ₹8,000 to ₹20,000, and a dynamic small business website ₹20,000 to ₹50,000. E-commerce and custom websites cost more. Check whether hosting, domain and SEO are included in the quote. See [WitLabs website pricing](/pricing/) for a worked example.

### Which is the best website design company in Bangalore?

The best company is the one that fits your business, not the one with the biggest ads. Shortlist two or three, compare their live work for businesses like yours, and ask the [7 questions in this guide](#7-questions-to-ask-before-hiring-a-website-design-company-in-bangalore).

### What is the difference between a website design company and a web development company in Bangalore?

A website design company focuses on how the site looks and how visitors move through it. A web development company builds the technical side, such as booking systems, dashboards or online stores. Most small businesses need both, so look for a team that does design and development together.

### Can I get affordable website design in Bangalore without losing quality?

Yes. A focused 5-page business website with mobile design, a WhatsApp button and basic SEO does not need a large budget. Spend on what brings enquiries, and skip features you will not use.

### How long does it take to build a business website?

A simple 5-page website can be ready in 3 to 7 days once you share your content. Larger or custom websites usually take several weeks.

### Should I hire a freelancer or a website design company?

Freelancers often charge less but may not offer long-term support. A company usually costs a little more but gives you a team, a written scope and help after launch.

### Will my new website show up on Google?

It can, if basic SEO and your Google Business Profile are set up properly. New websites usually take a few months to rank. No company can guarantee a first-page position.

### Do I need a website if I already have Instagram or a Google listing?

Yes. Instagram and Google listings help people discover you. A website is where they check if you are trustworthy and contact you, and it is the only online space you fully own.
