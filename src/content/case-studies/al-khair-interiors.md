## The Challenge

Al Khair Interiors had strong offline credibility, but almost no digital footprint. Their best projects were hidden inside phone galleries and word-of-mouth conversations, which made it difficult for new prospects to judge the quality of their work before getting in touch.

In a category like interior design, trust is built visually. When homeowners, builders and commercial clients searched online, competitors with polished websites and Google profiles looked more established, even when Al Khair Interiors had the stronger portfolio.

The business needed a digital presence that made their craftsmanship easy to discover, easy to understand and easy to act on.

## Our Approach

WitLabs built a premium portfolio-led website around the way interior design clients make decisions. The structure focused on project visuals, service clarity, trust signals and direct inquiry paths through forms and WhatsApp.

Alongside the website, we set up and optimized their Google Business Profile so local searchers could find accurate business information, see portfolio highlights and contact the team quickly. The SEO foundation targeted location-aware interior design searches with clean page structure and metadata.

The goal was not just to make the business look better online. It was to turn online discovery into qualified conversations.

## The Results

Al Khair Interiors now has a professional digital presence they can confidently share with prospects, partners and referral leads. Their website acts as a proof asset, while local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization help them appear where clients are already searching.

The result is a clearer inquiry journey, stronger first impression and better visibility against local competitors.
